# Henry Meyer — Software Development & Hardware Projects

Henry Meyer is a self-taught software developer and hardware enthusiast from Germany. He builds practical software and electronics projects, with an interest in maintainable code, thoughtful interfaces, open source, and hardware that can be repaired and expanded.

## About

Henry's work spans software development, Minecraft tools, digital accessibility, and modular hardware. He learns by building projects and sharing the results. His portfolio links to project pages, source repositories, and official project sites. Project descriptions summarize work in progress and do not imply that every project is a finished product or commercial service.

## Projects

- **Akku-Craft** — A sustainable, modular, and decentralized battery system designed to reduce electronic waste through decentralized monitoring and precise cell balancing. Henry contributes to hardware engineering and software development as a co-founder. In 2026, the team won first place at the Jugend forscht regional competition in Lingen and exhibited at IdeenExpo in Hanover. [Project details](https://henrymeyer.de/projects/akku-craft/) · [Website](https://akku-craft.eu) · [GitHub](https://github.com/akku-craft)
- **HMLabs Gaming** — Henry's independently developed and maintained collection of Minecraft mods, plugins, modpacks, and resource packs. He handles development, compatibility testing, documentation, release management, and shared release workflows across platforms such as Modrinth, CurseForge, Hangar, SpigotMC, and Planet Minecraft. [Project details](https://henrymeyer.de/projects/hmlabs-gaming/) · [Website](https://gaming.hmlabs.eu) · [GitHub](https://github.com/HMLabs-Gaming)
- **Projekt Ansichtssache** — A student accessibility project by students of Gymnasium Oesede. Henry developed the first prototypes, although he does not attend the school, and now maintains the browser extension, websites, and GitHub organization while Anton and Raphael lead more of the widget development. [Project details](https://henrymeyer.de/projects/projekt-ansichtssache/) · [Website](https://projekt-ansichtssache.github.io/) · [GitHub](https://github.com/Projekt-Ansichtssache) · [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/accessibility-widget/kffkafdmlcmmiefggbniofjmlkoiplkm) · [Firefox Add-ons](https://addons.mozilla.org/de/firefox/addon/accessibility-widget-by-p-a/)
- **HMLabs** — Henry's umbrella for independently developed and maintained open-source projects across software, developer tools, and Minecraft. [Project details](https://henrymeyer.de/projects/hmlabs/) · [Website](https://hmlabs.eu) · [GitHub](https://github.com/HMLabs-HQ)
- **Awesome AI for Beginners** — A curated collection of resources for people learning about AI without a technical background. [GitHub](https://github.com/henrymmey/awesome-ai-for-beginners)

## Skills and interests

Henry works with web technologies including HTML, JavaScript, and TypeScript, and uses tools such as Git, GitHub, GitHub Actions, Docker, Arduino, Bash, and PowerShell. He is interested in software engineering, hardware design, Minecraft modding, accessibility, and making technical topics easier to approach.
