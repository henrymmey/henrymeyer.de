## Overview

Akku-Craft is a sustainable, modular, and decentralized battery system designed to reduce electronic waste. Through decentralized monitoring and precise cell balancing, the project aims to provide a safe and long-lasting power supply for a wide range of devices.

## Projects

- **ArduinoBMS** — Arduino firmware for the battery management system
- **Schematics** — Hardware schematics and electronic circuit designs
- **3D Models** — CAD and 3D design files for the enclosure

## What I Do

As part of the team, I contribute to hardware engineering and software development. My work connects the physical battery system with the digital components, including firmware, monitoring, and the supporting electronics.

I work together with the team to turn concepts into prototypes, document technical decisions, and improve the system step by step.

## What I Learn

Akku-Craft gives me practical experience at the intersection of hardware and software. I learn about battery management systems, embedded development, electronics, hardware design, and the challenges of making physical products reliable and maintainable.

The project also teaches me how to work on a shared technical system, communicate across different areas of development, and consider safety, sustainability, and usability throughout the design process.

## Competitions and Events

In 2026, we won first place at the Jugend forscht regional competition. We later presented Akku-Craft at the Lower Saxony state competition at Clausthal University of Technology, where we did not receive a placement.

We also exhibited the project at IdeenExpo in Hanover for ten days, sharing our work with visitors and gaining experience presenting a technical project to a broad audience.

We plan to participate in Jugend forscht again in 2027.
