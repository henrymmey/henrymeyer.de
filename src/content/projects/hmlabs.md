## Overview

HMLabs is my home for independent open-source projects across software, developer tools, and Minecraft. It brings together the projects I build, maintain, and publish under one umbrella.

## Projects

- **HMLabs Gaming** — Minecraft mods, plugins, modpacks, and resource packs
- **@henrymmey/badges** — A collection of badges for projects, profiles, and communities

## What I Do

At HMLabs, I develop and maintain all projects independently. I take care of everything from the initial idea and technical planning to implementation, testing, documentation, releases, and ongoing maintenance.

The projects cover software, developer tools, and Minecraft, including mods, plugins, modpacks, resource packs, and web projects. Working across these areas means that I regularly switch between different technologies and responsibilities.

## What I Learn

Running HMLabs on my own gives me experience with the complete lifecycle of a project. I learn how to make technical decisions, structure code for long-term maintenance, automate repetitive work, and turn rough ideas into reliable, usable products.

It also teaches me to prioritize, manage releases, write useful documentation, and consider the needs of users and contributors. Since I am responsible for every part of the process, I continuously improve both my technical skills and my ability to organize and communicate my work.
