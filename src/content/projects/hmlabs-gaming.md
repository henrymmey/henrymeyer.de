## Overview

HMLabs Gaming is my collection of Minecraft projects, developed and maintained as part of HMLabs. The focus is on reliable, well-designed, and enjoyable projects for the Minecraft community.

## Projects

- **Modpacks** — HM Basic Play, JS Combat Pro, HM Life+, JS Performance Core, and HMT Pack
- **Resource packs** — JS Green Ores, JS Cyan Ores, and JS Removed Crosshair
- **Mods** — Attribute PVP Helper Reforged, HM Scoreboard Toggle, and Better Ping Display Reforged
- **Plugins** — HMTAPI for Minecraft server networks

## What I Do

I handle the complete development process independently, from designing gameplay features and writing code to testing compatibility, preparing documentation, and managing releases. I publish projects across platforms such as Modrinth, CurseForge, Hangar, SpigotMC, and Planet Minecraft.

I also maintain shared release workflows to automate recurring tasks and keep releases consistent across projects.

## What I Learn

Working on different kinds of Minecraft projects teaches me how to balance technical constraints with gameplay and user experience. I learn to design for different loaders, versions, servers, and communities while keeping projects maintainable over time.

Managing the projects independently also gives me practical experience with versioning, compatibility testing, release automation, and responding to feedback from players and server owners.
