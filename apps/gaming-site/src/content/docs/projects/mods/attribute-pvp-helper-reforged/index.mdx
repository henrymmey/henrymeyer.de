A small PvP helper for Minecraft 26.2 that gives you instant feedback during combat – all without changing your settings or adding anything to your hotbar.

> This is a continuation of the original [Attribute PVP Helper](https://modrinth.com/project/D7LBHzLK) by ViratS-best, now updated to Minecraft 26.2.

## What it does

- **Damage numbers** – Whenever you hit an enemy, the exact damage you dealt appears in your action bar. Same for damage you take, so you always know how much a hit really hurt.
- **Spear lunge helper** – No more guessing when a spear lunge works. The mod tells you when to charge, when you need forward momentum, and shows a clear _READY TO LUNGE_ the moment all conditions are met.
- **Weapon swap detection** – Switch weapons in your hotbar and you'll instantly see a confirmation of what you swapped to. Great for trying weapon combos mid-fight.
- **Mace range indicator** – Holding a mace? The mod shows you the distance to the nearest enemy, so you always know if your target is within reach.

## How to use

Just install the mod and join a world or server. Everything is shown directly in the action bar – nothing to configure, no keybinds to set up, no menus to open.

## What it _doesn't_ do

- No item, block or command additions
- No cheats, no latency tricks, no server authority
- All feedback is computed from what your client can already see

## Compatibility

- Minecraft **26.2** (Fabric)
- Works in singleplayer and on any multiplayer server – only you need the mod
- No server-side installation required
