A simple texture pack that turns the desired normal and deepslate ore blocks completely cyan. No other changes are made. Perfect for much better visibility and compatible with all Minecraft versions.

Recommended for players with red-green colorblindness (Protanopia & Deuteranopia) for maximum visibility and contrast.

<Aside type="tip">
  We also have these resource packs in green: [JS Green
  Ores](/projects/resourcepacks/js-green-ores/)
</Aside>
